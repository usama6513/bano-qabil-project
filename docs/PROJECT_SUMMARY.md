# EduGuard AI — Complete Project Summary

## 🎯 What Is EduGuard AI?

EduGuard AI is Pakistan's **first all-in-one education, finance, and security platform** built for students. It combines 6 specialized AI agents with real databases to guide students through every step of their academic and financial journey — while protecting them from fraud.

**Live URL:** [eduguard-ai-steel.vercel.app](https://eduguard-ai-steel.vercel.app)

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL (Neon), Groq AI + Gemini fallback, Vercel deployment

## 🛡️ Module 3: Fraud Detection & Protection

### How Fraud Protection Works:

EduGuard has a **multi-layer fraud detection system**:

#### 1. URL Scanner
- Paste any suspicious website URL
- AI checks for phishing patterns, fake scholarship sites, fraudulent universities
- Cross-references with known scam databases
- Returns risk score with detailed analysis

#### 2. Phone Number Scanner
- Enter any phone number (mobile + landline)
- Checks against known scam numbers in Pakistan
- Identifies if number is linked to fraud reports
- Shows carrier, location, and risk level

#### 3. Document Scanner
- Upload suspicious documents (admission letters, scholarship offers, fee receipts)
- OCR extracts text from images
- AI analyzes for forgery signs, fake logos, incorrect formats
- Compares with authentic document patterns

#### 4. Text/Message Scanner
- Paste any suspicious SMS, WhatsApp message, or email
- AI detects common scam patterns (urgency, fake links, too-good offers)
- Identifies phishing attempts targeting students

#### 5. Fraud Reporting System
- Users can **report new fraud** they encounter
- Reports include: type, description, evidence, contact info
- Reports are stored and used to warn other users
- Admin can review and take action

#### 6. Fraud Trends Dashboard
- Shows latest fraud patterns in Pakistan
- Common scam types targeting students
- Geographic distribution of fraud
- Time-based trend analysis

### Common Student Frauds in Pakistan:
- **Fake admission offers** from non-recognized institutions
- **Scholarship scams** asking for "processing fees"
- **Fake job/internship offers** requesting bank details
- **Phishing websites** mimicking HEC, universities, or banks
- **SMS scams** with malicious links
- **Fake degree/certificate sellers**
- **Unrecognized "consultants"** charging huge fees

## 📋 How to File a Fraud Complaint

### Through EduGuard AI Platform:
1. Go to **Fraud Detection** section in sidebar
2. Click **"Report Fraud"**
3. Fill in:
   - Type of fraud (fake university, scholarship scam, phishing, etc.)
   - Description of what happened
   - Evidence (screenshots, URLs, phone numbers, documents)
   - Your contact info (optional)
4. Submit — report is stored and reviewed

### Through Official Pakistani Authorities:

| Authority | For | How to Contact |
|-----------|-----|----------------|
| **FIA Cyber Crime Wing** | Online fraud, phishing, cyber scams | [nr3c.gov.pk](https://nr3c.gov.pk) or Helpline: **9911** |
| **HEC (Higher Education Commission)** | Fake universities, degree scams | [hec.gov.pk](https://hec.gov.pk) or Helpline: **111-99-432** |
| **FBR (Federal Board of Revenue)** | Tax fraud, fake NTN | [fbr.gov.pk](https://fbr.gov.pk) |
| **SECP** | Investment scams, fake companies | [secp.gov.pk](https://secp.gov.pk) |
| **SBP** | Banking fraud, fake banks | [sbp.org.pk](https://sbp.org.pk) |
| **Local Police** | Physical fraud, in-person scams | Helpline: **15** |
| **PTA** | Spam calls, SMS scams | [pta.gov.pk](https://pta.gov.pk) or **333** |

### Steps to Protect Yourself:
1. **Verify** any university on HEC's recognized list before paying fees
2. **Never pay** "processing fees" for scholarships — real scholarships are free
3. **Check URLs** carefully — fake sites often use slight misspellings
4. **Don't share** CNIC, bank details, or OTP with unknown callers
5. **Use EduGuard's scanners** before clicking links or sharing documents
6. **Report** any suspicious activity immediately
